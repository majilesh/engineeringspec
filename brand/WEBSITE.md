# EngineeringSpec website identity

The website is a compact documentation-led public surface, not a separate marketing product.

Every canonical page uses:

- the outlined horizontal lockup in navigation;
- Forest, Cloud, Ink, and restrained mint signal tokens;
- locally served Sora and Inter WOFF2 subsets;
- the canonical descriptor and supporting line;
- the 1200×630 mark-first Open Graph image;
- visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior;
- direct links to the `majilesh/engineeringspec` repository, guides, Explorer, and draft specification.

The homepage may explain the team-scale cost of discovering scope disagreement after a large generated diff. It must not imply that EngineeringSpec itself analyses architecture, finds duplicate tables or services, generates a design, writes code, or proves trusted checks passed.

GitHub avatar and social-preview updates are manual repository-setting steps performed only after the implementation assets have landed and rendered correctly.
